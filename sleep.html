Sleep and Rest

Good sleep is essential for focus, learning, and mental health. University life can disrupt sleep patterns, but small changes make a big difference.

Healthy Sleep Habits

  • Keep regular sleep and wake times, even on weekends.
  • Limit caffeine and screen use an hour before bed.
  • Make your room calm, dark, and cool.
  • Try relaxation or gentle stretching before sleeping.
  • Use your bed only for rest — avoid studying in it.

If poor sleep continues for several weeks, speak to your GP or wellbeing adviser — persistent insomnia can affect your mental health.
