Motivation

It’s normal for motivation to fluctuate during university. Low motivation can stem from tiredness, overwhelm, or perfectionism. Identifying what’s holding you back helps you move forward.

Ways to Boost Motivation

  • Break tasks into small, manageable steps and celebrate progress.
  • Set realistic goals and use a to-do list you can actually complete.
  • Work with others — shared accountability helps momentum.
  • Change your study environment to refresh focus.
  • Remind yourself why you started — reconnect with your long-term goals.

If you feel stuck or detached for a long time, reach out to student support. Talking about it often helps rebuild motivation and direction.
