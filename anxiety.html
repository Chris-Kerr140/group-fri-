Immediate Assistance

Anxiety

Everyone feels anxious at times, but if those feelings become overwhelming or constant, it’s important to reach out for help. Anxiety can affect sleep, focus, and confidence — but it is treatable and manageable.

Self-Management Strategies

  • Use slow breathing or 5-4-3-2-1 grounding to calm your body.
  • Challenge anxious thoughts by asking what evidence supports them.
  • Limit caffeine and alcohol — both can heighten anxiety symptoms.
  • Try gentle exercise such as walking or yoga to release tension.
  • Connect with friends or student wellbeing services for support.

If anxiety interferes with your studies or daily life, contact RGU Counselling or your GP. You don’t have to cope alone.
