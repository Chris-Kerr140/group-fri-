Alcohol Awareness

Many students enjoy drinking as part of social life, but excessive alcohol can impact mood, concentration, sleep, and safety. Understanding your limits and making mindful choices can help you stay balanced and in control.

Tips for Sensible Drinking

  • Know the recommended limit — 14 units a week, spread evenly over several days.
  • Have alcohol-free days and alternate with soft drinks.
  • Eat before and during drinking to slow absorption.
  • Look after friends — never leave anyone alone or vulnerable.
  • Seek help early if you’re using alcohol to cope with stress or emotions.

When to Get Help

If drinking is affecting your studies, relationships, or wellbeing, talk to student support, your GP, or a counsellor. Support is confidential and available to everyone.
