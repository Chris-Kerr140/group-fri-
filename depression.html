Immediate Assistance

Depression

Depression can affect how you think, feel, and function. It’s more than low mood — it can include fatigue, loss of interest, poor concentration, or hopelessness. Recognising symptoms is the first step to recovery.

Looking After Yourself

  • Stay connected with friends and family — isolation makes mood worse.
  • Maintain a routine: regular sleep, meals, and outdoor time.
  • Exercise gently — even short walks improve mood chemicals.
  • Limit alcohol and substances; they can deepen low feelings.
  • Be kind to yourself — progress takes time.

If you experience thoughts of self-harm or suicide, please seek help immediately — contact emergency services, Samaritans (116 123), or NHS 24 (111).
