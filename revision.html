Revision & Exams

Exams and coursework can be stressful, but preparation and planning help reduce anxiety. Balancing study time with breaks and rest supports both performance and wellbeing.

Study Strategies

  • Plan revision early — create a timetable that includes breaks.
  • Work in short bursts (e.g., 45 minutes) with 10–15 minute breaks.
  • Use active techniques: test yourself, summarise, and explain concepts aloud.
  • Mix subjects to stay engaged and prevent burnout.
  • Sleep and eat well — your brain performs best when rested and nourished.

If exam pressure feels overwhelming, talk to your tutor or student wellbeing adviser. RGU offers academic skills support and counselling services if needed.
