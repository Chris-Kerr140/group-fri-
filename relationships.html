Relationships

Healthy relationships contribute to happiness, confidence, and overall wellbeing. Communication, respect, and boundaries are key to maintaining positive connections with others.

Tips for Healthy Relationships

  • Communicate openly and honestly — say what you feel and listen to others.
  • Respect each other’s personal space, opinions, and privacy.
  • Recognise warning signs of unhealthy relationships, such as control, jealousy, or manipulation.
  • Spend time apart as well as together — independence is healthy.
  • Seek help if a relationship feels unsafe or emotionally draining.

Good relationships take work and understanding. If you’re struggling, RGU support services and national helplines can offer guidance and safety planning.
