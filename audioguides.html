Audio Guides for Wellbeing

The NHS offers free audio guides to help you manage stress, anxiety, and low mood. Each guide is led by a mental health professional and can be listened to anywhere you feel comfortable.

How to Use

  • Choose a topic that fits how you feel (e.g., Anxiety Control, Sleep, Low Mood).
  • Find a quiet space, sit comfortably, and listen through headphones or speakers.
  • Repeat the sessions to reinforce positive habits and coping skills.
