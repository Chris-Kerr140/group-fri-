Immediate Assistance

Self Harm

Self-harm is a way some people express deep distress. You are not alone, and help is available. Speaking to someone can begin the process of recovery and finding safer coping mechanisms.

Steps You Can Take

  • If you are in danger, call emergency services (999) or go to A&E.
  • Remove objects that may cause harm and reach out to someone you trust.
  • Try grounding or breathing exercises to manage overwhelming emotions.
  • Contact RGU Counselling, your GP, or charities that specialise in support.

Remember: you deserve help and support. Talking about it is a sign of strength, not weakness.
