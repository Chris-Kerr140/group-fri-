Immediate Assistance

Suicidal Thoughts

If you’re thinking about ending your life, please remember that help is available right now. You don’t have to face this alone — speaking to someone can keep you safe and help you find hope again.

What to Do Right Now

  • Call 999 (or 112) if you are in immediate danger.
  • Contact Samaritans on 116 123 for free, 24/7 confidential support.
  • Talk to RGU Counselling or your GP for ongoing help and safety planning.
  • Stay with someone you trust until you feel safer — you don’t have to go through this alone.

Thoughts of suicide can pass with time and support. Reaching out shows courage and can be the start of healing.
