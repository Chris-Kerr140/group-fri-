Traumatic Events

Experiencing or witnessing a traumatic event can leave emotional scars and physical symptoms. It’s common to feel shock, fear, guilt, or confusion afterward. Recovery takes time and support.

Managing After Trauma

  • Give yourself time — reactions such as nightmares or flashbacks are normal.
  • Talk to trusted friends, family, or a counsellor about what happened.
  • Maintain a daily routine — it restores a sense of stability.
  • Avoid using alcohol or drugs to numb emotions.
  • Seek professional help if symptoms persist longer than a few weeks.

You are not defined by what happened to you. Specialist trauma counselling can help you process memories safely and regain control.
